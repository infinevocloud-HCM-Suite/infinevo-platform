Who can do this: the payroll officer, or anyone whose role lets them manage salaries.

1. Open People, then Employees, and open the employee.
2. Choose the Salary tab.
3. Click Set up salary, or Revise if a salary already exists.
4. Under Basics, enter the Annual CTC and the Effective from date, and Notes if you like.
5. Under Earnings, click Add earning for each part of the pay, and pick the Balancing earning that makes the total equal the CTC.
6. Under Statutory, check the PF account number, UAN and ESI number.
7. Check the Summary, which shows the estimated take-home, and click Save.
