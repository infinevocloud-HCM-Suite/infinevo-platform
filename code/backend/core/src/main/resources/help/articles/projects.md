Who can do this: HR, or a manager whose role lets them manage projects. A manager sees only the projects they manage.

1. Open HRMS, then Projects. Use Search projects or pick a Status to find one.
2. To add a project, click New project. Enter the Name and, if you like, Category, Description, Start date, End date, Priority, Budget and Manager. Click Save.
3. Click a project's name to open it. Change its status with the status list, drag the Progress slider, or click Edit to change the details. Delete removes the project.
4. On the Team tab, pick an Employee and the Assigned on date and click Assign. Click Remove on a row to take someone off.
5. On the Tasks tab, click New task. Enter the Title and pick the Assignee, Due date, Priority, Status and Estimate (hours). Click Save.
6. To change a task, click Edit on its row. Click Delete to remove it.
