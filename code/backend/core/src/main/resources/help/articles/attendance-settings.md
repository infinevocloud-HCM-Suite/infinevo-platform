Who can do this: HR, or anyone whose role lets them manage attendance.

1. Open HRMS, then Attendance settings. Using defaults at the top means the settings have not been saved yet.
2. Under Hours calculation, pick First in, last out or Every session.
3. Enter the Full-day minimum hours and the Half-day minimum hours. The half day must be below the full day.
4. Enter the Regularization window (days) and the Regularizations per month.
5. Turn Allow regularization without a session on or off.
6. Click Save.
