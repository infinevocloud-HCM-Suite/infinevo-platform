Who can do this: a manager, or anyone whose role lets them delegate approvals.

1. Open Approvals, then Delegations. The table shows each delegation's Delegator, Delegate Colleague, Valid From, Valid To, Flow Types and Status.
2. Click New Delegation.
3. In Create Approval Delegation, pick the Delegate Employee.
4. Pick the Delegation Period: the first and last day the colleague acts for you.
5. Pick one or more Flow Types, for example Leave, Overtime or Timesheet.
6. Click Create.
7. To end a delegation early, click the delete button on its row and click Yes. Only the person who made a delegation can revoke it.

A delegation shows as SCHEDULED before its first day, ACTIVE while it runs, and EXPIRED or REVOKED after.
