Who can do this: the payroll officer, or anyone whose role lets them see pay runs. Approving needs the right to approve pay runs. You decide and press Approve yourself; the AI helper can summarise the run but never approves it.

Open Payroll, then Payroll runs, and open the run. Its status should be COMPUTED.

1. If it shows Computation failed, read the reason, fix it and click Compute again.
2. If a yellow box says there are new pay inputs since this run was computed, click Compute again. Approve is refused until you do.
3. Check Period, Pay Date, Included Employees and Skipped Employees. Is everyone you expect to pay included?
4. Check Total Gross, Total Deductions and Total Net Pay against last month. Ask why if one moved a lot.
5. In Employees in this Run, choose Skipped. The Skip Reason says why: no salary structure in force, or no bank details. Who is skipped is fixed when the run is created, so computing again does not add them. Fix their record and pay them with an off-cycle run if needed.
6. Choose Included and look down the Net Pay column for any figure that is zero or below zero.
7. Click Lines on an employee to see their Earnings, Deductions, Benefits and Reimbursements, with Paid days, LOP days and Payable days. Check new joiners, leavers and anyone with loss of pay.
8. To check in a spreadsheet, click Export to Excel.
9. When all is right, click Approve and confirm. Approving freezes the figures; the run can no longer be recomputed.
