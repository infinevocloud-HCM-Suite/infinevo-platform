Who can do this: HR, or anyone whose role lets them read all overtime.

1. Open HRMS, then All overtime.
2. Pick a Range of at most 93 days, for example Last 93 days or This month. Nothing loads until a range is picked.
3. Pick one Employee, or leave it as All employees.
4. The table shows each request's Employee, Date, Hours, Amount, Status, Source and Posted period.
5. Click a row to open the Overtime request. If the request is waiting on you, click Approve or Reject, add a Comment (needed to reject) and confirm.

Requests waiting on you are also in Approvals. See the article "Approve or reject a request".
