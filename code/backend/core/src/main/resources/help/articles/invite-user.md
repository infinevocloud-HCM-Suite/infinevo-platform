Who can do this: the tenant admin, or anyone whose role lets them manage users.

To invite a new person:
1. Open People, then Users & access, and choose the Invitations tab.
2. Click Invite user.
3. Enter the Email address and pick their Roles. You can leave Roles empty and give them later.
4. Click Send invitation. The invitation shows as Pending until the person accepts it.

To change what an existing person can do:
1. On the Users tab, find the person with Search by name or email.
2. Click Change roles, pick the roles and click Save.
3. To stop someone signing in, click Disable and confirm. Their roles are kept, and Enable lets them back in.
