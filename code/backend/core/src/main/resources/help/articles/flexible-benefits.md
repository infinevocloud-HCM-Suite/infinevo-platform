Who can do this: the payroll officer, or anyone whose role lets them manage payroll settings.

1. Open Payroll, then Payroll settings, and choose the Flexible Benefit Plan tab.
2. Turn on Enable Flexible Benefit Plan to let employees send their declarations while the window is open.
3. Pick Window Opens On and Window Closes On. Both are needed when the plan is on.
4. Under Notifications & Reminders, choose Notify Employees on Window Release and Notify Employees on Lock.
5. In Reminder Days Before Window Closes, type each day (1 to 60) and press Enter, for example 15, 7, 3, 1.
6. Click Save FBP Plan.
7. When declarations are done, click Lock Plan. Click Unlock Plan to open it again.
8. FBP Eligible Components lists the components employees can choose. To add one, click Manage in Salary Components and mark it as an FBP component there.
