Who can do this: the payroll officer, or anyone whose role lets them read reimbursement claims. Approving or rejecting is done by whoever the claim is waiting on in Approvals.

1. Open Payroll, then Reimbursement claims. The table shows each claim's Employee, Component, Bill date, Requested, Approved, Status and Posted period.
2. To narrow the list, pick an Employee, a Status (Submitted, Approved or Rejected) or a Bill date range. Click Refresh to read the list again.
3. Click a row to open the claim. It shows the Limit, the Requested amount, the Description and the Remarks.
4. To decide a claim that is Submitted, click Open the approval, or open Approvals, then Inbox.
5. Click Approve or Reject. To approve, enter the Approved Amount (INR); it can be less than the amount asked for. To reject, a Comment is needed.
6. Click Approve or Reject to confirm. An approved claim is paid in the pay run of its Posted period.

The helper explains these steps but does not approve or reject a claim for you. That decision stays on the screen.
