Who can do this: the payroll officer or the tenant admin, or anyone whose role lets them manage payroll settings.

1. Open Payroll, then Payroll settings, and choose TDS.
2. Turn Deduct TDS in pay runs on or off, and click Save. Off pauses TDS for everyone; each employee's settings are kept and apply again when it is turned back on. Until it is saved once, TDS is deducted by default.
3. The link below shows for how many employees TDS is applicable; click it to open the employee list.
4. Choose TDS deductor. Enter the TAN, PAN of the company, TDS circle and Deductor type, and the Company email, Company phone and GSTIN (optional).
5. Under Person responsible (signatory), enter the Name, Father's / parent's name, Designation, PAN, Email and Mobile.
6. Click Save.

Form 16 prints the TAN, PAN and signatory. The quarterly TDS return also needs the contact details. Its address comes from the work location marked as the filing address.
