Who can do this: the payroll officer, or anyone whose role lets them verify tax declarations.

1. Open People, then Employees, and open the employee.
2. Choose the Salary tab and find the TDS card. Pick the Financial year.
3. The card shows Annual gross, Taxable income, Annual tax, Regime, Effective month, Source and Note, then Deducted so far, Remaining, Months left and the next month's share. History lists earlier figures.
4. To set the figure yourself, click Edit. Enter the Annual tax, the Effective month it applies from and a Note saying why, and click Save.
5. Your figure stays until you change it. When a later declaration, proof check or salary revision works out a different figure, the card shows it as a suggestion. Click Apply suggestion and confirm to use it from the next pay run, or Clear suggestion to keep yours.

You change the figure yourself; the AI helper does not.
