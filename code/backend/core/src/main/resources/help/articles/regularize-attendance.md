Who can do this: any employee whose role lets them read their own attendance.

1. Open HRMS, then Regularisations.
2. Click New request.
3. Pick the Date, enter the In and Out times, and type a Reason.
4. Click Submit. The request goes for approval and shows in the list with its Status.
