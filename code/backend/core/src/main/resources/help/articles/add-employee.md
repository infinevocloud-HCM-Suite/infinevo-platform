Who can do this: HR, or anyone whose role lets them create employees.

1. Open People, then Employees.
2. Click New Employee. The Create New Employee page opens.
3. Fill in the required fields: Employee Number, First Name, Last Name, Work Email and Date of Joining.
4. Pick the Department, Designation and Work Location if you know them. Click More for Gender, Mobile, Employment Type, Probation End Date and Notice Period (days).
5. To let the person sign in, turn on Give portal access and pick their Roles. An email goes to the work email.
6. Click Create Employee. Then click Open employee page to add the rest of their details.
