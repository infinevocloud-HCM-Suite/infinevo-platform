Who can do this: the payroll officer, or anyone whose role lets them manage payroll settings.

1. Open Payroll, then Payroll settings.
2. The first time, click Launch Guided Setup Wizard and answer its questions for EPF, ESI and professional tax. Click Complete Statutory Setup at the end.
3. To change one later, choose its tab:
   - EPF: turn on Enable EPF, enter the EPF Registration Number, check the rates and wage ceiling rules, and click Save EPF Settings.
   - ESI: turn on Enable ESI, enter the ESI Registration Number, and click Save ESI Settings.
   - Professional Tax: the slabs come from each state of your work locations. Click Override only if your state's slabs differ.
   - TDS: choose whether to Deduct TDS in pay runs and click Save.
