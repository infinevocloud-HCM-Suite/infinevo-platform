Who can do this: any employee whose role lets them fill timesheets.

1. Open HRMS, then Timesheets. My timesheets lists your weeks.
2. Click Open this week, or click a week in the list. Use Previous week and Next week to move.
3. Click Add row, pick the Project and the Task, and enter hours for each day.
4. Click Save draft to keep your work.
5. Click Submit and confirm. A submitted week cannot be changed.
6. If a project is rejected, change those rows and click Resubmit.
