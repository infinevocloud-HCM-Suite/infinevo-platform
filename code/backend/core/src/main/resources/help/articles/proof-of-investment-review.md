Who can do this: the payroll officer, or anyone whose role lets them review proof. Others can only view.

1. Open Payroll, then Proof of investment. Pick the year under FY. The tags at the top count the proofs by status and say whether the proof window is open and when proof is due.
2. The Verification queue tab lists the submitted proofs. Search by Name or employee number, or change the status filter.
3. Click Review on a row. Each item shows what was Declared, Claimed and Verified. Click Open receipt to read the receipt.
4. For each item, click one of these and then Confirm:
   - Verify: enter the Verified amount. It cannot be more than the claim. If it is less, say why.
   - Disallow: give a Reason. The employee reads it.
   - Return: give a Reason. The whole proof goes back to the employee to correct and send again.
5. When the items are decided, click Approve proof at the bottom, or Return proof to send it back.
6. The Chase list tab shows who has not sent proof yet. If no reminder is set, click Set up reminder. Enter how many days before the due date to start, the Send at time and Repeat every, and click Save reminder.

The helper explains these steps but does not verify or disallow an amount for you.
