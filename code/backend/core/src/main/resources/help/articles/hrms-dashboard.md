Who can do this: anyone whose role lets them see their own projects, their team's projects or timesheets, or approve timesheets. Each card shows only when your role allows it.

1. Open HRMS, then Dashboard. The date under the title is when the figures were taken.
2. Today shows whether you are clocked in and how long you have worked. Click Attendance to clock in or out.
3. Timesheets shows this week and last week, with their status and hours. Click a week to open it.
4. My projects and My tasks show your Active projects and your Open, Overdue and Due this week tasks. Click My work for the full list.
5. Under Team, Projects I manage lists each project's Team, Open tasks and Overdue tasks.
6. Waiting for me counts the timesheets waiting on you and lists the oldest. Click one to review it, or click Go to approvals.
7. My team today shows your Reports, how many have Clocked in, and how many were Late last week.
