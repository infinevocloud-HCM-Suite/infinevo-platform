Who can do this: the payroll officer, or anyone whose role lets them read pay runs.

1. Open Payroll, then Payroll dashboard. Pick the financial year at the top.
2. If payroll setup steps are left, a note says how many. Click Open setup to finish them.
3. Current run shows the month's Status, Pay date, Included and Skipped employees, Gross, Deductions and Net pay. Click Open run to work on it. If there is no run yet, click Go to pay runs.
4. Employees shows who is Active today and, for the last run, who was included and who was skipped, with the reason.
5. The EPF, ESI, Professional tax and TDS tiles show the year's employee and employer amounts from paid runs.
6. This year lists each month's Gross, Deductions, Tax and Net pay, with the paid total at the bottom. Recent runs lists the latest runs. Click a month to open its run.
