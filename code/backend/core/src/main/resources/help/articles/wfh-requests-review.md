Who can do this: HR, or anyone whose role lets them read all work-from-home requests or their team's.

1. Open Work from home, then WFH requests.
2. Use the tabs to filter: All Requests, Pending Approval, Approved, Rejected or Cancelled.
3. Click View on a row to see the WFH Application Details and its Approval Trail.
4. To apply for someone, click Apply on Behalf. Only those whose role lets them manage work-from-home see this button.
5. Pick the Employee. Turn on Half Day Application for a half day and pick the Date and Period, or pick the Date Range for full days.
6. Enter the Reason and click Submit on Behalf. A request made on behalf is approved when it is created.

Approving or rejecting a request is done from Approvals. See the article "Approve or reject a request".
