roles: tenant-admin
screen: /settings/company
keywords: company profile, logo, tagline, company name, branding, header
---
Who can do this: the tenant admin.

1. Open Settings, then Company profile.
2. The Company name is set when the company was created and is shown here.
3. Type a Tagline, the line under the company name, or leave it empty.
4. Under Logo, upload your logo, or click Remove logo.
5. Click Save.
