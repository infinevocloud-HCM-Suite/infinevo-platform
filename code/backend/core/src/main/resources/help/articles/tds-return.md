Who can do this: the payroll officer, or anyone whose role lets them manage tax. Others can look and download, but not change anything.

1. Open Payroll, then Quarterly TDS return. Pick the Financial year and the quarter (Q1 Apr–Jun to Q4 Jan–Mar).
2. Click Record challan. Enter the Financial Year, Quarter, BSR Code (Bank Branch), Challan Serial No, Tender / Deposit Date and Minor Head, then the Tax and any Surcharge, Education Cess, Interest and Fee / Penalty (Sec 234E). Check the Total Deposited Amount and click Record challan.
3. In Challans, click Allocate on the challan. It lists each employee's TDS from the quarter's paid runs. Enter the amount under This challan, or click Fill remaining, and click Save. The challan's Status shows Fully allocated, Partly allocated or Not allocated.
4. Check the figures at the top: TDS deducted, Covered by challans, Not yet covered (should be zero) and Employees without PAN.
5. Under Download, click Statement (CSV) to check the quarter. If a box says Before the portal file can be made, fix each point listed; Deductor details opens the company's TDS details.
6. Click Form 138 file for the portal (.txt). Check it with Protean's File Validation Utility (FVU 1.2) before uploading it. In Q4, Year's salary statement (CSV) is also offered.
7. After filing, click Mark filed, enter the 15-digit Token number from the acknowledgement and the filed date, and click Mark filed. The quarter is then locked; click Reopen only to make a correction.

You record, allocate and file yourself; the AI helper does not change challans or file the return.
