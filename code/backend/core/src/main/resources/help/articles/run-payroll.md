Who can do this: the payroll officer, or anyone whose role lets them run payroll. Each button shows only to those allowed to use it.

1. Open Payroll, then Payroll runs. Pay Runs lists every run with its Status.
2. Click New Run, choose the month under Select Pay Period and create it. It starts as DRAFT.
3. Open the run. Check loss of pay and other inputs first, then click Lock and confirm. Locking freezes pay inputs.
4. Click Compute and confirm. When it shows COMPUTED, check Total Gross, Total Deductions and Total Net Pay, and the employees.
5. Click Approve and confirm. Approved figures can no longer be recomputed.
6. Click Download Bank File to get the salary payment file for your bank.
7. After the bank pays, click Pay, enter the Date paid and click Mark paid. Payslips are then ready for employees.
