Who can do this: the payroll officer, or anyone whose role lets them export bank files. You press every button yourself; the AI helper does not make or download the file.

The bank file buttons show only once the run is APPROVED or PAID.

1. Open Payroll, then Payroll runs, and open the run.
2. Click Bank file….
3. Under Template, pick your bank's template, or the built-in one. Templates are set up under Payroll settings, Bank files.
4. Under Employees, choose Everyone in this run, or Only the ones I tick and tick the employees. You can search by number or name.
5. Click Preview. Check Rows in file, File total and Run net pay (or Ticked net pay). Anyone left out is listed with the reason, such as missing bank details; pay them another way or fix their bank details.
6. Click Make file. The file downloads straight away and is kept on the run.
7. To get a file again later, find it in the Bank files list on the run page and click Download.

Download Bank File, next to Bank file…, gives the plain CSV in one click. If anyone is left out or the file total is less than the run's net pay, it warns you first; click Download Anyway only if you mean to.
