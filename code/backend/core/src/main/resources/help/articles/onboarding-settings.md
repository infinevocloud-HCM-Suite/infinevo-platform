Who can do this: the tenant admin, HR, or anyone whose role lets them manage onboarding.

1. Open Settings, then Onboarding templates. The table lists each task with its Order, Title, Type, Assigned Role, Due Days and Status.
2. Click Add Template.
3. Enter the Title and, if you like, a Description. Pick the Task Type: Document Upload, Profile Update, Policy Acknowledgement or Manual Task. For Document Upload, also pick the Document Label.
4. Pick the Assigned Role (Employee, HR, Manager or IT) and enter the Due Days After Joining. Set Mandatory Task, then click OK.
5. To change a task, click Edit. Use the up and down arrows under Reorder to change the order. Click Deactivate so new joiners no longer get a task, or Activate to bring it back.
6. Each employee you add gets every active task, due the set number of days after their date of joining. Employees loaded from an import file do not.
7. To give the checklist to someone who has none, open the employee, choose the Onboarding tab and click Assign Default Checklist. There you can also Approve, Reject, Complete or Waive each task.
