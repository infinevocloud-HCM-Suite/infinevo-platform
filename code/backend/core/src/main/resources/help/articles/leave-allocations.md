Who can do this: HR, or anyone whose role lets them manage leave allocations.

1. Open Leave, then Leave allocations. The table shows each employee's Entitlement, Accrued, Carried Forward, Consumed and Remaining days.
2. To give someone days by hand, click Allocate.
3. In Manual Leave Allocation, pick the Employee, the Leave Type and the Leave Year, and enter the Opening Days.
4. Click Save Allocation.
5. To credit the days that have been earned so far, click Run Accrual As Of, pick the Accrual As Of Date and click Run Accrual.
