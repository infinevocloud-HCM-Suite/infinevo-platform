Who can do this: any employee with a self-service page.

1. Open My self-service, then the My Leave tab.
2. Click Apply for Leave.
3. Pick the Leave Type, the From Date and the To Date. For half a day, turn on Half Day and pick the Period.
4. Type a Reason, and add a document under Supporting Documents / Attachments if you need to.
5. Click Submit to send it for approval, or Save Draft to finish later.
6. The request shows on My Leave with its status. While it is waiting you can open it and click Withdraw.
