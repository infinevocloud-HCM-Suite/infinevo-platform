Who can do this: HR, or anyone whose role lets them manage work-from-home allocations.

1. Open Work from home, then WFH allocations.
2. Pick the year at the top. The table shows each employee's Year, Validity Range, Entitlement, Used, Balance and Pro-rate.
3. To give someone a quota, click Allocate Quota.
4. In Allocate Annual WFH Quota, pick the Employee and enter the Allocation Year.
5. Set the Start Date and End Date, the Quota (Days) and, for someone who joined part way through, the Pro-rate Factor (1 means the full quota).
6. Click Save Allocation.

To load many quotas at once, use WFH import. See the article "Load work-from-home quotas from a file".
