Who can do this: HR, or anyone whose role lets them read all attendance.

1. Open HRMS, then All regularisations.
2. Pick a Range of at most 93 days, for example Last 93 days or This month. Nothing loads until a range is picked.
3. Pick a Status (PENDING, APPROVED or REJECTED) or leave All statuses, and pick one Employee or leave All employees.
4. The table shows each request's Employee, Date, In, Out, Reason, Status and Decided at.
5. Click a row to open the Regularization and see who decided it and their Decision comment.
6. If the request is waiting on you, click Approve or Reject, add a Comment (needed to reject) and confirm.

Requests waiting on you are also in Approvals. See the article "Approve or reject a request".
