Who can do this: any employee whose role lets them mark attendance.

1. Open HRMS, then Attendance. The Today card shows your day.
2. Click Clock in when you start work.
3. Click Clock out when you stop. You can clock in and out more than once in a day.
4. My days lists each Date with First in, Last out and Worked hours.

If you forgot to clock in or out, read the article "Correct a missed clock-in".
