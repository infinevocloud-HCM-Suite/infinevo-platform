Who can do this: the tenant admin.

1. Open Settings, then Setup. The Setup Checklist lists Core Setup, HRMS Setup and Payroll Setup steps.
2. Each step shows its state: To do, Done, Skipped, or Pre-filled - review.
3. Click Open on a step to go to its screen, finish it there and come back.
4. A Pre-filled - review step was filled in from the country template. Open it, check the values and save.
5. To leave a step out, click Skip, give a Reason for Skipping and click Confirm Skip.
