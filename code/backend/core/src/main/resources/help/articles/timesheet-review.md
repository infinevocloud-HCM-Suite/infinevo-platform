Who can do this: a manager or project manager whose role lets them approve timesheets.

1. Open HRMS, then Timesheet review.
2. Choose My projects, My team or All, and a Status.
3. Click a row to open that Employee's Week.
4. Check the hours per project and click Approve or Reject. A rejection needs a reason, which the employee sees.
