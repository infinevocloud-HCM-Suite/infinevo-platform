Who can do this: the payroll officer, or anyone whose role lets them read statutory reports. Uploading Part A also needs the right to make statutory reports.

1. Open Payroll, then Payroll settings, and choose Form 16. Pick the Financial Year at the top.
2. To make Part B, stay on Part B Statements. Under Select Employee search for the employee and click Generate Form 16.
3. Check the statement: deductor and employee details, the quarterly TDS summary and Part B salary and tax. Click Print Statement to print it or save it as a PDF.
4. If it says Tax Deductor Details Incomplete, fill in the company's TAN, PAN and TDS circle under Payroll settings, TDS deductor, and try again.
5. To add Part A, choose TRACES Part A Upload. Drag the Form 16 Part A zip you downloaded from TRACES into the box (a .zip up to 50 MB) and click Upload & Process ZIP.
6. Check the result: how many certificates were Matched to employees by PAN, Unmatched or Skipped. Uploaded certificates are listed below; click Download PDF to open one.

Employees see their own certificates under Tax declaration, on the Form 16 Certificates tab.
