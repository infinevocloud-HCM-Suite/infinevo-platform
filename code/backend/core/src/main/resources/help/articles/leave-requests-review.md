Who can do this: HR, or anyone whose role lets them read all leave.

1. Open Leave, then Leave requests.
2. Use Filter by employee and Filter by status (for example Pending Approval) and click Filter.
3. Click View on a row to see the Leave Request Details and its Approval Trail.
4. To record leave for someone, click Record Leave, fill in Employee, Leave Type, From Date and To Date, and click Record Leave.

Approving or rejecting a request is done from Approvals. See the article "Approve or reject a request".
