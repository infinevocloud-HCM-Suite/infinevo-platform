Who can do this: any employee asked to declare their tax savings.

1. Open My self-service, then the Tax declaration tab.
2. Check the Selected Regime. To change it, click Change Regime, choose the New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC) or the Old Tax Regime and click Apply Regime Change.
3. Fill in the sections that apply: Housing (HRA / Loans), Deductions & 80C, and Other Income. Save each section.
4. Check the Tax Summary.
5. Click Submit Declaration and confirm. Payroll then uses it to work out your monthly TDS.
6. To change it after submitting, click Reopen Declaration while that is still allowed.
