Who can do this: any employee with payslips.

1. Open My self-service, then the My Payslips tab. Each paid month shows its Period, Paid on date and Net pay.
2. Click View on a month.
3. Click Download / Print PDF to save or print it.

A payslip appears only after the month's pay run is marked paid.
