Who can do this: HR or payroll, whose role lets them enter pay inputs.

1. Open HRMS, then Loss of pay. The list shows the days entered by hand, which the pay run deducts.
2. Click Add loss of pay.
3. Pick the Employee and the Month, enter the Days and a Reason.
4. Click Save.
5. To undo one, click Reverse on its row, give the Reason for reversal and click Reverse.

Enter it before the month's pay run is locked.
