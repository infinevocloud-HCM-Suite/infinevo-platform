Who can do this: the payroll officer, or anyone whose role lets them verify tax declarations. Others can only see the list.

1. Open Payroll, then Tax recalculation failures. It lists the recalculations that failed after a declaration, proof or salary change, and have not worked since.
2. Pick a Financial year, or leave All years. Click Refresh to read the list again.
3. Read each row's Employee, Trigger and Reason. Attempts, First failed and Last failed say how often and when it failed.
4. Fix what the Reason names, for example the employee's salary or declaration.
5. Click Retry on the row. When it works, a message shows the annual tax and the row leaves the list. If it fails again, the row stays with the new Reason.
