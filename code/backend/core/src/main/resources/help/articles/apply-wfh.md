Who can do this: any employee with a self-service page and a work-from-home quota.

1. Open My self-service, then the Work From Home tab. Your balance is shown there.
2. Click Apply for WFH. The button is greyed out if HR has not given you a quota or you have no days left.
3. Pick the dates under Select Date Range, or turn on Half Day Application, pick one date and the Half Day Period.
4. Type the Reason for Remote Work.
5. Click Submit Application.
