## Every day
- Act on the requests that reach HR. {screen:core.approvals.inbox} {article:approve-request}
- Check who is on leave and who has asked for leave. {screen:core.leave.requests} {article:leave-requests-review}
- Check the work-from-home requests. {screen:core.wfh.requests} {article:wfh-requests-review}
- Check the attendance log for missed clock-ins. {screen:hrms.attendance_log} {article:attendance-log}
- Look at the requests to fix attendance. {screen:hrms.regularizations_all} {article:regularizations-review}

## Every week
- Keep the departments right. {screen:core.org.departments} {article:departments}
- Keep the designations right. {screen:core.org.designations} {article:designations}
- Keep the work locations right. {screen:core.org.locations} {article:work-locations}

## Before month end
- Enter each employee's loss of pay days for the month. {screen:hrms.loss_of_pay} {article:loss-of-pay}
- Check everyone's overtime before payroll. {screen:hrms.overtime_all} {article:overtime-review}

## When someone joins
- Add the new employee. {screen:core.employee} {article:add-employee} {action:core.employee.create}
- Add many employees at once from a file. {screen:core.employee} {article:import-employees} {action:core.employee.create}
- Keep the onboarding checklist for new joiners right. {screen:core.settings.onboarding} {article:onboarding-settings}

## Once a year
- Review the leave types and their rules. {screen:core.leave.types} {article:leave-types}
- Give everyone the new year's leave. {screen:core.leave.allocations} {article:leave-allocations}
- Load leave balances for many employees from a file. {screen:core.leave.import} {article:leave-import}
- Publish the year's holidays. {screen:hrms.holidays} {article:holidays} {action:core.holiday.manage}
- Review the work-from-home policies. {screen:core.wfh.policies} {article:wfh-policies}
- Give everyone the new year's work-from-home days. {screen:core.wfh.allocations} {article:wfh-allocations}
- Load work-from-home quotas for many employees from a file. {screen:core.wfh.import} {article:wfh-import}
- Review the attendance rules: full-day hours and regularization limits. {screen:hrms.attendance_settings} {article:attendance-settings}
