## Every day
- Clock in when you start work and clock out when you finish. {screen:hrms.attendance} {article:clock-in-out}
- Fill in today's hours on your timesheet. {screen:hrms.timesheets} {article:timesheets}

## Every week
- Submit the week's timesheet for approval. {screen:hrms.timesheets} {article:timesheets}

## When needed
- Apply for leave from My self-service. {screen:core.me} {article:apply-leave}
- Ask to work from home from My self-service. {screen:core.me} {article:apply-wfh}
- Ask to fix a missed or wrong clock-in. {screen:hrms.regularizations} {article:regularize-attendance}
- Check the holiday list. {screen:hrms.holidays} {article:holidays}

## Every month
- Read your payslip on My self-service. {screen:core.me} {article:view-payslip}

## Once a year
- Declare your tax savings and upload the proofs on My self-service. {screen:core.me} {article:tax-declaration}
