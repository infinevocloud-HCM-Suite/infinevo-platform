## At the start
- Finish the setup checklist. {screen:core.setup} {article:setup-checklist} {action:core.tenant.manage}
- Invite your HR and payroll staff and give them their roles. {screen:core.users} {article:invite-user}
- Check what each role can do. {screen:core.roles} {article:roles}

## When needed
- Keep the company profile right. {screen:core.settings.company} {article:company-profile} {action:core.tenant.manage}
- Set who approves what. {screen:core.approvals.definitions} {article:approval-definitions}
- Set up the onboarding checklist for new joiners. {screen:core.settings.onboarding} {article:onboarding-settings}
- Keep the bank file template matching your bank's upload. {screen:payroll.settings} {article:bank-file-templates}
- Keep the TDS switch and the company's deductor details right. {screen:payroll.settings} {article:tds-settings}

## Every month
- Read the audit log. {screen:core.audit} {article:audit-log}
